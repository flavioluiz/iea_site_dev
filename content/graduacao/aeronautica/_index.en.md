## About the Program

The Aeronautical Engineering program at ITA trains engineers with a solid scientific foundation and skills to design, develop, and operate aircraft and aeronautical systems. With emphasis on aerodynamics, structures, propulsion, flight mechanics, and aircraft systems, graduates are prepared to work in the aeronautical industry, research centers, and armed forces.

## Curriculum Structure

The curriculum comprises: (a) Required Courses, (b) Elective Courses, (c) Supervised Internship, and (d) Complementary Activities.

The professional course spans 3 years (6 semesters) and covers the core aeronautical engineering disciplines, culminating in the Graduation Work (TG-1 and TG-2).

## Graduate Profile

The aeronautical engineer trained at ITA is qualified to:

- Design and analyze aircraft and aeronautical components
- Work in computational and experimental aerodynamics
- Develop aeronautical propulsion systems
- Perform structural analysis and work with composite materials
- Integrate complex aircraft systems
- Lead aeronautical engineering projects

## Career Opportunities

- **Aeronautical industry:** Embraer, Boeing, Airbus, and suppliers
- **Defense:** Brazilian Air Force (FAB), Navy, DCTA
- **Research:** IAE, INPE, private R&D centers
- **Certification and regulation:** ANAC
- **Advanced air mobility startups (AAM/UAM)**
