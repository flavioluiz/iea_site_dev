The AER Division is responsible for part of ITA's undergraduate courses and activities, with a focus on solid training in aeronautical and aerospace engineering.

## Programs

ITA offers undergraduate degrees in **Aeronautical Engineering** and **Civil-Aeronautical Engineering**, with a 5-year duration. AER is responsible for most of the aeronautical and aerospace specialty courses.

[ITA Website — Undergraduate](http://www.ita.br/graduacao)

## Courses

The courses offered by AER cover the main areas of aeronautical and aerospace engineering:

- Aerodynamics and Fluid Mechanics
- Flight Mechanics and Control
- Aeronautical Structures
- Aeronautical and Space Propulsion
- Aircraft and Spacecraft Systems
- Instrumentation and Avionics
- Integrated Aircraft Design
