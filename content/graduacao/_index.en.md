The Division of Aeronautical and Aerospace Engineering (IEA) at ITA offers two 5-year engineering undergraduate programs, internationally recognized for their excellence in technical and scientific education.

## Programs Offered

### Aeronautical Engineering
Training focused on the design and development of aircraft and aeronautical systems. Covers aerodynamics, structures, gas propulsion, flight mechanics, aircraft systems, and integrated design.

**Coordinator:** Rafael Marques Lins

### Aerospace Engineering
Comprehensive training for the design and operation of complex space systems: satellites, rockets, launch vehicles, and probes. Created in 2010, it is the only Aerospace Engineering program in the Brazilian Armed Forces.

**Coordinator:** Maísa de Oliveira Terra

## Program Structure

Both programs last **5 years**, divided into:

- **Fundamental Course (2 years):** Common foundation in mathematics, physics, chemistry, humanities, and computing, shared with all ITA programs.
- **Professional Course (3 years):** Specialty courses, integrated design projects, and graduation work (TG-1 and TG-2).

## Graduate Studies

The Division also contributes to the graduate programs:

- **PG-EAM** — Graduate Program in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering
- **PG-CTE** — Graduate Program in Space Sciences and Technologies
- **MP-PEE** — Professional Master's in Aeronautical Engineering (Embraer partnership)
