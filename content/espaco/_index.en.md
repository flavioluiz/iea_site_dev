ITA plays a leading role in developing space technologies in Brazil. The AER Division concentrates expertise in space propulsion, launch vehicles, satellites, guidance, navigation and control (GNC) systems, and reentry technologies — in collaboration with DCTA, AEB, INPE, and international partners.

---

## Why Space?

Space dominance is strategic for Brazil. AER/ITA trains the engineers and researchers who develop the technologies needed for the country to achieve space autonomy — from satellite design to the development of national launch vehicles.

---

## Our Focus Areas

### Space Propulsion
Solid and liquid propellant engines, electric propulsion, micropropulsion systems for CubeSats.

### Satellites and CubeSats
Design, integration, and operation of small satellites. Participation in national and international missions.

### Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC)
Attitude and orbit control algorithms, inertial navigation, space sensors.

### Launch Vehicles
Rocket aerodynamics, launcher structures, stage separation systems.

### Remote Sensing
Satellite instrumentation, orbital image processing, environmental and defense applications.

### Atmospheric Reentry
High-temperature thermodynamics, thermal shields, reentry ballistics.
