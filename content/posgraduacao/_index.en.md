Faculty of the Division of Aeronautical and Aerospace Engineering are active in academic graduate programs (stricto sensu), a professional master's program, and specialization courses, covering aeronautical, space, and defense areas.

---

## Academic Graduate Programs (Stricto Sensu)

### PG-EAM — Graduate Program in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering

Academic master's and doctoral program focused on aeronautical and applied mechanical engineering. Research lines cover aerodynamics, structures, propulsion, flight mechanics, and aeronautical systems.

- **Level:** Master's and Doctorate
- **Area:** Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering
- **Website:** [www.ita.br/posgraduacao/pgeam](https://www.ita.br/posgraduacao/pgeam)

---

### PG-CTE — Graduate Program in Space Sciences and Technologies

Academic master's and doctoral program dedicated to the space area, with research lines in space systems, satellites, space propulsion, guidance and navigation, remote sensing, and applied astrophysics. The only space technologies graduate program in the Brazilian Armed Forces.

- **Level:** Master's and Doctorate
- **Area:** Space Sciences and Technologies
- **Website:** [www.cte.ita.br](https://www.cte.ita.br/)

---

## Professional Master's

### MP-PEE — Professional Master's in Aeronautical Engineering

Professional master's program developed in partnership with **Embraer**, aimed at training engineers for the aeronautical industry. Combines academic rigor with direct application in aircraft development projects.

- **Level:** Professional Master's
- **Partner:** Embraer
- **Website:** [embraer.com — EM Engenharia](https://www.embraer.com/corporate-careers/emengenharia/pt/)

---

## Specialization Courses

### Aviation Safety and Continued Airworthiness

Specialization course for civil and military aviation professionals, focusing on operational safety, human factors, accident investigation, and continued airworthiness.

- **Level:** Specialization
- **Area:** Aviation Safety
- **Website:** [www.safety.ita.br](https://www.safety.ita.br/)

---

### CASSA — Specialization in Weapon Systems Analysis and Selection

Specialization course for military personnel and defense engineers, focusing on methodologies for analysis, selection, and evaluation of aeronautical weapon systems.

- **Level:** Specialization
- **Area:** Aeronautical Weapon Systems

---

### CEEAA — Specialization in Aerial Armament Engineering

Specialization course in aerial armament engineering, covering weapon systems, integration with aeronautical platforms, ballistics, and guided munitions technologies.

- **Level:** Specialization
- **Area:** Aerial Armament Engineering
